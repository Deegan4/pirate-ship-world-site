Common questions

My progress isn't on my other device. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure iCloud Drive is on. Progress syncs when the game is opened with a network connection.

I bought something but didn't get it. Open the Marketplace and tap Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

I want a refund. Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The on-screen controls disappeared. They hide while a game controller is connected. Disconnect or turn off the controller to bring them back.
