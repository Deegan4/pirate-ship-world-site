Privacy Policy

Pirate Ship World · Last updated 29 October 2026

HomeSupport

Pirate Ship World is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We don't collect your personal information, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across apps or websites. There are no accounts and no analytics.

Game progress

Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved to your own private iCloud storage so they can sync between your devices. That data is held by Apple under your Apple Account. We can't see or access it.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details. The game keeps a record of what you've bought, on your device and in your iCloud storage, so your items can be restored.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service under your Game Center profile. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage and delete Pirate Ship World's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
